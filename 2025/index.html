Model United Nations 2025

11th to 13th October 2025

Dear MUN directors, advisors, delegates, student officers, admins, and guests,

On behalf of the RijnMUN 2025 Board of Directors, welcome to the archive of RijnMUN 2025.
The second RijnMUN great success. We thank everyone who was present and made it possible.
Below, you will find a record of the second edition of RijnMUN.
